常見問題

存檔放在哪裡？

存在你的裝置裡（App 自己的儲存空間；網頁版是瀏覽器裡這個網站的資料），不用註冊帳號，也沒有雲端備份。刪掉 App 或清除網站資料，存檔就跟著不見；要搬到別台裝置，請用下面的「換手機」。

換手機怎麼把存檔搬過去？

在舊裝置打開「設定 → 🎒 換手機 → 舊手機產生碼」，會得到一組 10 碼轉移碼；在新裝置打開象棋千秋 →「設定 → 🎒 換手機 → 新手機・輸入轉移碼」輸入。轉移碼 30 分鐘內有效。存檔在傳送時用這組碼加密，新裝置收到後伺服器上那一份馬上刪除；不想傳了可以按「作廢這組碼」。

怎麼刪除我的資料？

  • 伺服器上的資料：設定 →「🔒 系統・隱私・家長」→ 隱私「🗑 申請刪除我在伺服器上的資料」；或寫信到上面的信箱，主旨寫「刪除資料」，信裡附上裝置代號（不用寫名字）。裝置代號在設定房間書案上的驛路文牒「🔒 隱私說明」裡，可以按「📋 複製」。開發者會在 30 天內處理。
  • 裝置裡的資料：設定裡的「🗑 刪除這支手機的全部資料」，或直接刪掉 App。
  • 請先申請刪除伺服器上的資料，再刪裝置裡的（裝置資料刪掉後裝置代號會換新，就對不到以前送出的資料了）。會刪哪些、哪些匿名資料刪不到，見隱私權政策的「怎麼刪除」。

線上大廳遇到不舒服的事，檢舉多久會處理？

點對方的名字，資料卡最下面有「🚩 檢舉」和「🚫 封鎖」；封鎖馬上生效，對方不會知道是誰檢舉的。檢舉由開發者本人逐筆人工處理，目標在 7 天內處理（例如停權）；處理進度可以在「設定 → 角色 → 線上大廳 → 🚩 我的檢舉」看。急事請同時寫信到上面的信箱，主旨寫「急」並附上檢舉編號（例如 R-1111-7K3Q）。家長也可以在「👪 家長與護眼」關掉線上大廳，或打開「👧 兒童模式」。

Frequently asked questions

Where is my save stored?

On your device (in the app's own storage; on the web, in your browser's data for this site). There is no account and no cloud backup. Deleting the app or clearing the site's data removes the save; to move it to another device, use "Move to a new phone" below.

How do I move my save to a new phone?

On the old device, open Settings → "🎒 Move to a new phone" → "Old phone: get code" to get a 10-character transfer code. On the new device, open Xiangqi Qianqiu → Settings → "🎒 Move to a new phone" → "New phone: enter code" and type it in. The code is valid for 30 minutes. The save is encrypted with the code while in transit, and the copy on the server is deleted as soon as the new device receives it; tap "Cancel This Code" if you change your mind.

How do I delete my data?

  • Data on our servers: Settings → "🔒 System · Privacy · Parents" → Privacy → "🗑 Request deletion of my data on the server", or email the address above with the subject "Delete data" and your device ID (no name needed). The device ID is under "🔒 Privacy" on the Courier Pass on the desk in the Settings room; tap "📋 Copy". The developer handles it within 30 days.
  • Data on the device: "🗑 Delete all data on this phone" in Settings, or simply delete the app.
  • Please request server deletion first (after the device data is deleted the device ID changes, and earlier submissions can no longer be matched). For what is deleted and which anonymous data can't be, see "How to delete your data" in the Privacy Policy.

Something bad happened in the online lobby. How soon are reports handled?

Tap the other player's name; the profile card ends with "🚩 Report" and "🚫 Block". Blocking takes effect immediately, and the other player isn't told who reported them. Reports are reviewed one by one by the developer, with the aim of handling each within 7 days (for example, a suspension). You can follow progress in Settings → Character → Online lobby → "🚩 My reports". For urgent cases, also email the address above with "Urgent" in the subject and the report number (for example R-1111-7K3Q). Parents can also turn off the online lobby or turn on "👧 Kids Mode" in "👪 Parents and eye breaks".

Does it cost anything?

No. It is completely free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
